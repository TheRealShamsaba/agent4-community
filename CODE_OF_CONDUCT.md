# Code of conduct

Make this a useful and welcoming place for people learning to build companies and software.

Be respectful, explain your reasoning, and help people ask questions without embarrassment. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and spam are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation. Report sensitive conduct issues through the repository's private reporting channel without posting personal details publicly.
